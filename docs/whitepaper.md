# Ringlaunch Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Vision: an ecosystem where the people who keep a token are its core participants rather than its exit liquidity.

Mission: give creators a simple path onto Solana, and steer activity around each token toward its keepers, openly and in terms anyone can read.
